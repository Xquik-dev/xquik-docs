| Field                   | Type       | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`                    | `string`   | X's ID for the play within the game.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `sequence`              | `string`   | The play's place in its drive, as X writes it.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `clock`                 | `string`   | The game clock when the play started, as `HH:MM:SS`.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `type`                  | `string`   | X's kind of play, such as `Pass`, `Rush`, `Punt` or `Conversion`.                                                                                                                                                            |
| `direction`             | `string`   | Where the play went, such as `Left` or `Middle`.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `description`           | `string`   | The play as the official play-by-play writes it.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `scoring`               | `boolean`  | Whether the play scored points.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `homePoints`            | `number`   | The home team's points after the play.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `awayPoints`            | `number`   | The away team's points after the play.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `score`                 | `object`   | For a scoring play, its `points` & the `homePoints` & `awayPoints` after it. Null otherwise.                                                                                                                                 |
| `start`                 | `object`   | Down & distance when the play started, or null.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `start.down`            | `number`   | The down, from 1 to 4.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `start.yardsToGo`       | `number`   | The yards needed for a first down.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `start.yardline`        | `number`   | The yard line of the ball.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `start.yardlineSide`    | `string`   | Whose half the ball was in. `Home` or `Away` in NFL games, the team's `alternateId` in college games.                                                                                                                        |
| `start.possession`      | `string`   | The team with the ball. `Home` or `Away` in NFL games, the team's `alternateId` in college games.                                                                                                                            |
| `onField`               | `object`   | Flags X shows on the field, or null. `calledBack` means a penalty called the play back. `penaltyOnField` means a flag was thrown. `timeout` means a timeout followed. `underReview` means officials were reviewing the play. |
| `details`               | `object[]` | The play's steps, in order, such as a pass, a tackle or a penalty.                                                                                                                                                           |
| `details[].sequence`    | `number`   | The step's place in the play, from 1.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `details[].category`    | `string`   | X's kind of step, such as `Pass`, `Tackle` or `Penalty`.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `details[].description` | `string`   | What happened in the step.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `details[].direction`   | `string`   | The direction X gives for the step.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `details[].yards`       | `number`   | The yards the step gained or lost.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `details[].players`     | `object[]` | The players in the step. Each has `playerId`, a key of `players`, & `playerRole`, such as `Pass`, `Receive` or `Tackle`.                                                                                                     |
| `details[].penalty`     | `object`   | The penalty this step called, or null. It has `description`, `nextDown`, `result`, `yards` & `teamId`, the penalized team.                                                                                                   |
| `post`                  | `object`   | The post X links to the play, with `id` & the author's `username`. Null when X links none.                                                                                                                                   |
| `videoPostId`           | `string`   | The ID of a post with the play's video.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `videoReplyPostId`      | `string`   | The ID of a reply with the play's video.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
