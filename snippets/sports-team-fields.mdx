<ResponseField name="id" type="string">
  The team ID that games & players refer to.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="name" type="string">
  The team's full name, such as `San Francisco 49ers`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="shortName" type="string">
  The team's short name.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="abbreviation" type="string">
  The team's abbreviation, such as `SF`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="alternateId" type="string">
  Another ID X keeps for the team. College plays & drives name a side by it.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="location" type="string">
  The school or place the team plays for. College schedules & a game's audience send it.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="colors" type="object[]">
  The team's colors. Each has `r`, `g` & `b`, from 0 to 255.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="logos" type="object">
  The team's logo links. `default` is the one X shows. `light` & `dark` suit light or dark
  backgrounds.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="xUserId" type="string">
  The user ID of the team's X account. Pass it to [Get
  user](/api-reference/x/twitter-profile-lookup).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="record" type="object">
  The team's `wins`, `losses` & `ties` this season. X sends it only in college football games.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="recentGames" type="object[]">
  The team's recent games, newest first. Games without a kickoff time come last. Each has `id`,
  `startsAt`, `venue`, `status`, `clock`, `quarter`, `home` & `away`. `home` & `away` hold `teamId`
  & `points`. X sends them only with the teams of a college football game. The list is empty
  elsewhere.
</ResponseField>
