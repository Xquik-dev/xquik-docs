## <span className="nw">Source-backed</span> Xanguard scope

Xanguard's official home page advertises sub-second Twitter/X alerts for crypto. It lists delivery through Telegram, REST API, and HMAC-signed webhooks. It also lists WebSocket streaming on B2B plans.

{"\n"}

The home page lists 14 REST API endpoints and 4 delivery channels. It also lists HMAC-SHA256 webhook signatures, 8 products, and a free tier. It lists plans from USD 19/month paid in SOL.

{"\n"}

Xanguard's public workflow describes adding accounts and detecting new tweets. It also describes applying keyword filters, mute rules, and reply and repost exclusions. It covers contract detection and alert delivery too. Alerts go through Telegram, webhooks, REST clients, or B2B WebSocket.

{"\n"}

The official B2B page describes 4 real-time modules in one connection. They cover tweets, follows, profile changes, and community monitoring. The page says tweet events include replies, quotes, and retweets. They include full untruncated text, media URLs, quoted tweet content, and contract address extraction.

{"\n"}

B2B setup starts with Telegram bot or REST API target creation. Next comes a WebSocket connection at `wss://api.`<span className="cn"><wbr />`xanguard.`<wbr />`tech/v1/dt/`<wbr />`realtime/ws`</span>. That connection uses API-key login through an opcode protocol.

{"\n"}

B2B pricing lists RT 25 at USD 49/month for 25 tracked accounts. It lists RT 100 at USD 149/month for 100 tracked accounts. It also lists RT 500 at USD 499/month for 500 tracked accounts. Enterprise has custom pricing above 500 accounts.

{"\n"}

The B2B plan cards include RT 25, RT 100, and RT 500. Each of those cards lists WebSocket, REST API, webhook delivery, and Telegram delivery. Each also lists all 4 real-time modules. RT 100 adds priority support.

{"\n"}

The page also says you pay with SOL or Telegram Stars. Per the page, Xanguard activates the plan after confirming payment.
