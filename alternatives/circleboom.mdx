Circleboom is a Twitter management tool. It deletes tweets, manages followers and schedules posts. This page lists its Twitter plans as its pricing page showed them. It then maps each Circleboom job to an Xquik tool.

<Info>

Check [Circleboom's pricing page](https://circleboom.com/twitter-management-tool/plans-and-pricing) for its current plans before you compare.

</Info>

## Delete tweets in bulk

The [Tweet Deleter](https://xquik.com/en/tweet-deleter?ref=docs-circleboom) removes posts, replies and reposts from your connected X account. Find them by date, type, words, hashtag, likes or language. You can also paste post links. Mark the posts to keep, then confirm.

{"\n"}

X lists only your newest 3,200 posts. The deleter reads older posts too, with no archive upload.

{"\n"}

Each post it reads costs 1 credit. Each deleted post costs 10 credits. So 1,000 posts cost 11,000 credits. A write that needs a fresh X sign-in adds 10 credits. The USD 20 plan includes 140,000 credits a month.

{"\n"}

The [Tweet Unliker](https://xquik.com/en/tweet-unliker?ref=docs-circleboom) removes 1 like by its post URL. [Undo Retweets](https://xquik.com/en/tweet-unretweeter?ref=docs-circleboom) removes 1 repost the same way. For many, call the same actions through the API or MCP.

</section>

<section>

## See who unfollowed you on Twitter

The [Unfollowers Tracker](https://xquik.com/en/twitter-unfollowers-tracker?ref=docs-circleboom) labels each lost follower with a date and reason. Its first read saves your followers. The list fills from the next change.

{"\n"}

It names lost followers for accounts with up to 100,000 followers. Above that, it covers the newest followers only. It also lists who doesn't follow you back.

{"\n"}

Tracking runs as an account monitor at 30 credits per hour. That is 720 credits a day. Tracking needs no connected X account. Unfollow and remove actions need one.

</section>

## Move from Circleboom to Xquik

<Steps>

  <Step title="Connect your X account">

    Sign in to Xquik and connect the account on the [X accounts page](https://dashboard.xquik.com/en/x-accounts). Read tools need no connected account.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Clean up posts and follows">

    Delete posts in the Tweet Deleter. Unfollow or remove followers in bulk from the Unfollowers Tracker.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Start tracking">

    Press Start tracking in the Unfollowers Tracker. Lost followers show up from the next change.

  </Step>
