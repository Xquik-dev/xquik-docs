## <span className="nw">Source-backed</span> Hypefury scope

Hypefury's pricing page lists Starter, Creator, Business, and Agency plans with 7-day trials. Starter costs USD 29/month. It schedules up to 1 month for 6 social accounts, including 1 X account. It also covers long posts, autoplugs, cross-posting, thread hooks, and templates. Starter covers Auto-DM, engagement tools, Gumroad sales, and support too.

{"\n"}

Creator is USD 65/month. It includes scheduling up to 3 months and 250 Auto-DMs/day. It covers 30 social accounts with 5 X accounts. It also includes 10 Tweet-to-Reels automations/month and weekend support. Its engagement builder access is for 100 users and 10 keywords. Business is USD 97/month with 60 social accounts, 10 X accounts, and 300 Auto-DMs/day. It also has 50 Tweet-to-Reels automations/month and engagement builder access for 20 keywords. Agency is USD 199/month with 90 social accounts, 15 X accounts, and 400 Auto-DMs/day. It also has 300 Tweet-to-Reels automations/month and engagement builder access for 50 keywords.

{"\n"}

Hypefury no longer offers a free plan, but offers a 7-day trial on Starter. The same official page says so. It says Hypefury supports Instagram business accounts, LinkedIn posts and carousels, Threads, and X/Twitter. Its plan comparison lists Instagram, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn, Threads, and TikTok.

{"\n"}

Hypefury has an official publish page. It describes creating content once, scheduling it, and distributing it to other social channels. It lists inspiration from 15+ hand-curated niches, 30+ tweet templates, and recurrent posting plans. It also lists a minimal editor.

{"\n"}

The official Auto DMs help page says DMs run in batches every 30 minutes. It says campaigns last 3 days and the number of DMs/day depends on plan. It also says edited tweets can break Auto DM because the tweet ID changes. It says DM recipients must follow the account.

## Migration path

Start with one publishing or reporting task. Validate exports, API access, monitoring, and alerts in Xquik. Keep the content calendar stable while you do.

{"\n"}

Keep the test small. Use 1 task, 1 output, 1 cost model, and 1 owner for the output. Switch only when the Xquik output matches the required records. It must also reduce integration work or cost.

{"\n"}

Check live Hypefury pricing and Xquik pricing because public plans change.

{"\n"}

Price the real workload. On Xquik, Starter is USD 20/month with 140,000 credits. Top-ups are USD 0.00015/credit. Webhook and stored-event management cost no credits. Active monitors bill only while enabled.
