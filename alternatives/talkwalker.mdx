This guide compares Talkwalker and Xquik for the X part of a workflow. That can be a social listening, consumer intelligence, or social media analytics workflow. Compare tasks like tweet search, follower export, account or keyword monitoring, webhooks, and handoffs. A handoff sends records to apps, warehouses, or agents.

## Source-backed Talkwalker scope

Talkwalker's official social listening pages describe tracking keywords and mentions. The tracking spans 30 social networks, 150+ million websites, videos, and images. It also spans podcasts, reviews, surveys, and support interactions. Their public pages also describe visual listening, customizable dashboards, and real-time alerts. They describe conversation clusters and sentiment analysis too. They list AI summaries, virality maps, AI chat and answers, and customer feedback analytics.

Talkwalker's official product pages describe a consumer intelligence platform. It includes Social Listening, Social Benchmarking, Media Monitoring, and Customer Feedback Analytics. It also includes real-time social insights. Their data coverage pages describe 30+ social networks and 300+ review sites. They also describe over 150 million websites. Source categories include X, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Bluesky, Snapchat, Twitch, and Reddit. They also include search engines, news, and review sources.

Use those official pages to verify one side of the comparison. That side is broad social listening and consumer intelligence. Use Xquik's API reference and workflow guides to verify the X-specific side. That side covers tweet search, follower exports, monitors, webhooks, exports, SDKs, and MCP.

Choose Talkwalker for consumer intelligence and social listening across many sources. Choose Xquik when the X task needs direct records, repeatable API calls, or exports. It also fits when the task needs signed webhooks or agent access.

## What to test in Xquik

For social listening and consumer intelligence comparisons, run the trial on one X workflow. Compare returned records, export format, webhook delivery, API behavior, and cost.

Start with tweet search, follower exports, monitor events, or signed webhook payloads. CSV, JSON & XLSX exports, SDK calls, or MCP tool results also work.

<CardGroup cols={2}>

  <Card title="Search tweets" icon="search">

## What to verify in a trial

## Migration path

Start with one X-only workflow and one owner for its output. Leave the full consumer intelligence program in place.

1. Talkwalker covers broad social listening, consumer intelligence dashboards, and visual analysis. It also covers sentiment, trends, and competitive intelligence. Keep using it if your team already works that way.
2. Use Xquik for the X task that needs API output. Examples are tweet search, follower export, tweet monitoring, webhook delivery, SDK calls, and MCP.
3. Keep both systems side by side until the Xquik output fits the receiving system. It must match the fields, freshness, and format that system needs.

Price the real workload. On Xquik, Starter is USD 20/month with 140,000 credits. Top-ups are USD 0.00015/credit. Webhook and stored-event management cost no credits. Active monitors bill only while enabled.
