## Where TwitterAPIs fits

TwitterAPIs calls itself a pay-per-call Twitter and X API. A standard read costs USD 0.0008 and returns about 20 tweets. On a full page, that is USD 0.00004 per tweet, below Xquik's USD 0.00015.

This guide compares TwitterAPIs and Xquik on specific X tasks. It covers tweet search, writes, monitors, webhooks, SDKs & agent connections.

<Info>

Facts on this page come from TwitterAPIs' official pages, read on 2026-10-15. Verify current pricing and terms on the official site before buying.

</Info>

## Source-backed TwitterAPIs scope

- The pricing page lists USD 0.0008 per standard call. A read returns about 20 tweets.
- The pricing FAQ puts a full 20-tweet page at USD 0.00004 per tweet.
- TwitterAPIs measured its search pages at closer to 8 tweets. That is USD 0.0001 per tweet.
- The homepage says an empty call still counts as a billed call.
- Likes, retweets, bookmarks & follows cost USD 0.0008 per call. Creating a tweet or sending a DM costs USD 0.0016.
- New accounts get USD 0.50 in free credits. Credits never expire.
- Account monitoring includes 1 free account. Paid plans start at USD 15 a month for 6 accounts.
- The Starter monitor plan typically detects posts in under 60 seconds, by polling. Higher plans add 2-second delivery once a post is detected.
- Requests use a bearer key. The MCP server has 112 tools, hosted or run through npx.
- The homepage lists a Python package, an npm package & an OpenAPI 3.1 spec.

## What to test in Xquik

Price the whole task with your real queries. Count the tweets each call returns, including empty calls. Xquik costs less when a call returns 5 tweets or fewer. 5 rows cost USD 0.00075, under the USD 0.0008 call price. Full pages cost less on TwitterAPIs.

An always-on Xquik monitor costs USD 0.0045 an hour. That is USD 3.24 for 30 days. TwitterAPIs lists account monitors from USD 2.50 each a month, plus 1 free. Xquik checks every second and also watches keywords.

Xquik's X Tweet Scraper ran in public Apify benchmarks on 2026-09-27 & 2026-09-29. It cost USD 0.000175 per useful tweet, the lowest of 12 Apify Actors. It was also the fastest, at 39.2 useful tweets per second. The benchmark covers Apify Actors, not TwitterAPIs. Read the [published benchmark](https://xquik.com/en/trust#benchmark-x-tweet-scraper).

Run the same queries in both products. Compare tweets per call, empty calls, fields & cost. Move the task once the systems that read the data accept Xquik's output.
