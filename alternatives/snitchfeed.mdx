## Why Xquik wins over SnitchFeed

Xquik is the fastest X data service, & the cheapest per useful result. Public Apify benchmarks ran on 2026-10-15. Xquik's X Tweet Scraper delivered 172.4 useful tweets per second, the fastest. It cost USD 0.000155 per useful tweet, the lowest of 5 Actors. Every run is public on the [benchmark page](https://xquik.com/en/trust#benchmark-x-tweet-scraper).

Xquik publishes reproducible benchmarks with run IDs. SnitchFeed publishes none.

Xquik bills only delivered, unique, filter-matching results. Empty pages, failed requests, retries, duplicates & filtered rows cost nothing. [SnitchFeed's credit docs](https://snitchfeed.com/docs/credits) bill 2 credits each time a keyword's X scan runs. They bill per search, not per post found. Starter sells 7,000 credits for USD 59 a month, about USD 0.0084 each. So 1 X scan costs about USD 0.0169, even when it finds nothing. On Xquik, a search that keeps 3 tweets costs USD 0.00045. That makes Xquik the cheapest per useful result.

Xquik also covers more of X in 1 product. It offers 23 extraction tools, 18 write actions & 1-second monitors with signed webhooks. Agents connect through a remote MCP server, & developers use SDKs in 8 languages.

## Where SnitchFeed fits

SnitchFeed is an AI social listening tool for startups & B2B SaaS teams. It scores mentions for relevance, intent & sentiment. On Starter & Pro, it scans X hourly.

This guide compares SnitchFeed & Xquik on X monitoring. It covers scan speed, billing, delivery, writes & agent connections.

## Source-backed SnitchFeed scope

- SnitchFeed watches LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Bluesky & more.
- X & LinkedIn scan hourly on Starter & Pro. Scale scans in real time.
- Starter costs USD 59 a month, or USD 47 a month billed yearly. It includes 7,000 credits.
- Pro costs USD 119 a month, or USD 95 a month billed yearly. It includes 21,000 credits.
- Scale starts at USD 399 a month.
- An X keyword scan or X API search costs 2 credits.
- AI scoring costs 0.5 credits per stored mention.
- The X search API returns up to 20 posts per call.
- Alerts go to email, Slack & Discord. Webhooks & CSV exports need Pro.
- Every plan includes the API & MCP.
- New accounts get 1,000 free credits with no card.
