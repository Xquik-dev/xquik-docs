## Why Xquik wins over Pulsar

Xquik is the fastest X data service, & the cheapest per useful result. Public Apify benchmarks ran on 2026-10-15. Xquik's X Tweet Scraper delivered 172.4 useful tweets per second, the fastest. It cost USD 0.000155 per useful tweet, the lowest of 5 Actors. Every run is public on the [benchmark page](https://xquik.com/en/trust#benchmark-x-tweet-scraper).

Xquik publishes reproducible benchmarks with run IDs. Pulsar publishes none.

Xquik bills only delivered, unique, filter-matching results. Empty pages, failed requests, retries, duplicates & filtered rows cost nothing. [Pulsar's site](https://www.pulsarplatform.com/) lists no prices & offers demos instead. Xquik publishes every price. A tweet costs USD 0.00015, & a search that returns 0 tweets costs 0. That makes Xquik the cheapest per useful result.

Xquik also covers more of X in 1 product. It offers 23 extraction tools, 18 write actions & 1-second monitors with signed webhooks. Agents connect through a remote MCP server, & developers use SDKs in 8 languages.

## Where Pulsar fits

Pulsar calls itself an audience intelligence & social listening platform. It collects social, search, news & web data from 195 countries.

This guide compares Pulsar & Xquik on X data. It covers mentions, raw rows, alerts, writes & agent connections.

## Source-backed Pulsar scope

- Pulsar's products include TRAC, CORE, TRENDS, Crisis Oracle, CLEAR & Narratives AI.
- It collects social, search, news media & web data from 195 countries.
- Its named social sources include Twitter, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram & Pinterest.
- It also covers regional sources such as VK, Naver, Weibo, WeChat & Baidu.
- TRAC offers over 50 filters, plus templated or custom dashboards.
- Pulsar says its AI detects sentiment, topics & emotions in all languages.
- Audience segmentation shows the main communities in a conversation.
- Pulsar also sells research & consultancy services.
- The site lists no prices. It offers demos instead.
