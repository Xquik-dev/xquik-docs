## Why Xquik wins over Brand24

Xquik is the fastest X data service, & the cheapest per useful result. Public Apify benchmarks ran on 2026-09-27 & 2026-09-29. Xquik's X Tweet Scraper delivered 39.2 useful tweets per second, the fastest. It cost USD 0.000175 per useful tweet, the lowest of 12 Actors. Every run is public on the [benchmark page](https://xquik.com/en/trust#benchmark-x-tweet-scraper).

Xquik publishes reproducible benchmarks with run IDs. Brand24 publishes none.

Xquik bills only delivered, unique, filter-matching results. Empty pages, failed requests, retries, duplicates & filtered rows cost nothing. [Brand24's pricing](https://brand24.com/prices/) sells fixed monthly plans with mention caps. Individual costs USD 249 a month for 2,000 mentions. At full use, that is USD 0.1245 per mention. On Xquik, 2,000 tweets cost USD 0.30. That makes Xquik the cheapest per useful result.

Xquik also returns full tweet text. [Brand24's API page](https://brand24.com/social-listening-api/) says X text fields come back empty.

Xquik also covers more of X in 1 product. It offers 23 extraction tools, 18 write actions & 1-second monitors with signed webhooks. Agents connect through a remote MCP server, & developers use SDKs in 8 languages.

## Source-backed Brand24 scope

- Brand24's sources include X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, news & blogs.
- Plans cost USD 249, USD 349, USD 499 & USD 699 a month.
- Annual billing lowers Individual to USD 199 a month.
- Enterprise starts at USD 1,499 a month, billed annually.
- Monthly mention caps run from 2,000 on Individual to 100,000 on Business.
- Individual updates every 12 hours. Team updates every hour. Pro & Business update in real time.
- AI sentiment analysis labels mentions positive, negative or neutral.
- Alerts go to email, Slack & Teams. A mobile app is also available.
- API access costs extra on Business. Enterprise includes it.
- The API returns empty text fields for X posts, citing platform terms.
- API pages hold up to 500 rows. Each date range covers up to 31 days.
- Brand24 also lists an MCP connection for AI assistants.
- Reports export to PDF, Excel & PowerPoint.
- The first 14 days are free, with no credit card.
