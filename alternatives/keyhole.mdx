## Why Xquik wins over Keyhole

Xquik is the fastest X data service, & the cheapest per useful result. Public Apify benchmarks ran on 2026-09-27 & 2026-09-29. Xquik's X Tweet Scraper delivered 39.2 useful tweets per second, the fastest. It cost USD 0.000175 per useful tweet, the lowest of 12 Actors. Every run is public on the [benchmark page](https://xquik.com/en/trust#benchmark-x-tweet-scraper).

Xquik publishes reproducible benchmarks with run IDs. Keyhole publishes none.

Xquik bills only delivered, unique, filter-matching results. Empty pages, failed requests, retries, duplicates & filtered rows cost nothing. [Keyhole's plans page](https://keyhole.co/select-plan/) lists Team & Enterprise plans with post caps & no prices. Its pricing link opens a demo request. A historical X report starts at USD 50. On Xquik, 10,000 tweets from a past date range cost USD 1.50. That makes Xquik the cheapest per useful result.

Xquik also covers more of X in 1 product. It offers 23 extraction tools, 18 write actions & 1-second monitors with signed webhooks. Agents connect through a remote MCP server, & developers use SDKs in 8 languages.

## Where Keyhole fits

Keyhole sells social listening, profile analytics & hashtag tracking. Its social listening is now available on Muck Rack.

This guide compares Keyhole & Xquik on X hashtag & keyword data. It covers tracking, history, exports, writes & agent connections.

## Source-backed Keyhole scope

- Keyhole offers social listening, profile analytics & hashtag tracking.
- Its social listening is available on Muck Rack.
- The plans page lists Team & Enterprise with no prices.
- Team includes 5 topics, 10k posts, 10 profiles & 3 users.
- Enterprise includes 15+ topics, 20k posts & historical data.
- Team reports export to PDF & XLS.
- Historical X reports start at USD 50, based on the volume of tweets analyzed.
- A historical report arrives within 1 business day, in PDF & Excel.
- One-time historical reports have no trial. Keyhole offers a sample report.
- The X hashtag analytics trial needs no credit card.
