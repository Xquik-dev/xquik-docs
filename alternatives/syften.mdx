## Why Xquik wins over Syften

Xquik is the fastest X data service, & the cheapest per useful result. Public Apify benchmarks ran on 2026-09-27 & 2026-09-29. Xquik's X Tweet Scraper delivered 39.2 useful tweets per second, the fastest. It cost USD 0.000175 per useful tweet, the lowest of 12 Actors. Every run is public on the [benchmark page](https://xquik.com/en/trust#benchmark-x-tweet-scraper).

Xquik publishes reproducible benchmarks with run IDs. Syften publishes none.

Xquik bills only delivered, unique, filter-matching results. Empty pages, failed requests, retries, duplicates & filtered rows cost nothing. [Syften's documentation](https://syften.com/documentation) sells X monitoring as a USD 40 monthly add-on for 1,000 tweets. AI-rejected posts still count toward that allowance. So each X post costs USD 0.04 there, kept or rejected. On Xquik, a kept tweet costs USD 0.00015. That makes Xquik the cheapest per useful result.

Xquik also covers more of X in 1 product. It offers 23 extraction tools, 18 write actions & 1-second monitors with signed webhooks. Agents connect through a remote MCP server, & developers use SDKs in 8 languages.

## Where Syften fits

Syften calls itself an AI-filtered keyword monitoring tool for the public web. X is a paid add-on. Its X alerts can arrive up to 15 minutes late.

This guide compares Syften & Xquik on X keyword alerts. It covers alert speed, billing, delivery, writes & agent connections.

## Source-backed Syften scope

- Syften watches Reddit, GitHub, forums, Bluesky, Mastodon & more.
- Plans cost USD 29.95, USD 49.95 & USD 119.95 a month.
- X monitoring is a USD 40 monthly add-on for Standard & PRO.
- The X add-on allows up to 65 filters & 1,000 tweets a month.
- Extra tweets cost USD 0.04 each. AI-rejected posts still count toward the allowance.
- X alerts arrive with a delay of up to 15 minutes.
- Turning on X monitoring requires signing in with an X account.
- Alerts go to email, Slack, RSS feeds & the API. Webhooks & MCP need PRO.
- The MCP server has 4 tools & cannot publish posts.
- Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial & no card.
