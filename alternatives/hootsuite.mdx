## Source-backed Hootsuite scope

Hootsuite has an official plans page. It lists Standard with up to 10 social accounts and unlimited scheduling. Standard includes posting-time suggestions, AI images and captions, and Canva and Adobe Express templates. It also includes one inbox and DM automations. It also includes 7-day mention search, sentiment analysis, 5 competitor benchmarks, and DM assignments.

Hootsuite's Advanced plan adds unlimited social accounts, custom analytics reports, and saved replies. It adds automated responses and bulk scheduling for up to 350 posts. It also adds message routing, tagging, 20 competitor benchmarks, and report delivery. Advanced adds 30-day mention search and outbound post tags too. The plans page lists PDF, PPT, CSV, XLSX, scheduled email, and calendar exports.

Hootsuite's publishing pages describe list and calendar views, plus AI posts and images. They also describe custom approvals, task assignments, posting-time suggestions, and Whiteboard planning. Hootsuite's inbox tools cover comments, DMs, saved replies, and automated replies. They also cover routing, agent collisions, and customer surveys. Social listening covers trends, topics, mentions, hashtags, sentiment, and websites. Enterprise options include SSO, advanced analytics and inboxes, and employee advocacy. They also include review management, compliance, chatbots, and Salesforce.

Hootsuite's plans page lists Standard at USD 99 per user/month with 10 social accounts. Advanced costs USD 249 per user/month and lists no social-account cap. Both prices use annual billing. Enterprise uses contact pricing. It adds custom access, support, advocacy, listening, analytics, inbox, Salesforce, and services. The page also lists a 30-day trial and a skip-trial discount.

## Migration path

Hootsuite covers cross-channel planning, inboxes, approvals, analytics, ads, and listening. Keep using it if your team already works that way. Move one X-only task when its output must become API data or files. Do the same when its output must become signed webhooks or MCP tool results.

Keep the test small. Use 1 task, 1 output, 1 cost model, and 1 owner for the output. Use Xquik for X work that needs tweet search, follower export, or post actions. It also fits media uploads, DMs, tweet monitoring, webhook delivery, SDK calls, or MCP.

Price the real workload. On Xquik, Starter is USD 20/month with 140,000 credits. Top-ups are USD 0.00015/credit. Webhook and stored-event management cost no credits. Active monitors bill only while enabled.
