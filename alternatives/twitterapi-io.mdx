## Where TwitterAPI.io fits

TwitterAPI.io calls itself a public social data API for developers & AI agents. Its pay-as-you-go tweet price matches Xquik's at USD 0.00015 per tweet. The 2 differ on empty results, subscriptions, writes, streams & agent tooling.

This guide compares TwitterAPI.io and Xquik on specific X tasks. It covers tweet search, follower exports, monitors, publishing, webhooks & agent connections.

<Info>

Facts on this page come from TwitterAPI.io's official pages, read on 2026-10-15. Verify current pricing and terms on the official site before buying.

</Info>

## Source-backed TwitterAPI.io scope

- Tweets cost 15 credits each, which is USD 0.00015 per returned tweet. USD 1 buys 100,000 credits.
- Every call costs at least USD 0.00015, also when it returns no data.
- On the USD 1,499 Business subscription, a tweet costs about USD 0.00008.
- A single profile lookup costs USD 0.00018. Batches of 100 or more profiles cost USD 0.00001 each.
- Followers cost USD 0.00001 to USD 0.00003 each, by page size. 200 per call is USD 0.00001 each.
- Posting a tweet, liking, following & other v2 writes cost USD 0.002 per call.
- Article content costs USD 0.001 per call. A media upload costs USD 0.003.
- The account stream starts at USD 29 a month for 6 accounts. Median delivery is under 500 ms.
- Keyword filter rules push tweets by webhook or WebSocket, at USD 0.00015 per tweet.
- Requests use 1 `x-api-key` header. The open-source MCP server runs locally with 12 read-only tools.
- Never-paid accounts get 0.2 requests per second. Paid accounts get 20, Scale 50 & Business 500.
- New accounts get USD 1 in free credits, valid for 31 days. Paid credits never expire.

## What to test in Xquik

Price the whole task. The pay-as-you-go tweet prices match. TwitterAPI.io costs less for bulk followers, bulk profiles, posting & Business plan tweets. Xquik costs less for empty searches, single profiles, likes, follows & articles.

Xquik's X Tweet Scraper ran in public Apify benchmarks on 2026-09-27 & 2026-09-29. It cost USD 0.000175 per useful tweet, the lowest of 12 Apify Actors. It was also the fastest, at 39.2 useful tweets per second. The benchmark covers Apify Actors, not TwitterAPI.io. Read the [published benchmark](https://xquik.com/en/trust#benchmark-x-tweet-scraper).

Run the same query in both products. Compare rows, empty pages, fields & cost. Move the task once the systems that read the data accept Xquik's output.
