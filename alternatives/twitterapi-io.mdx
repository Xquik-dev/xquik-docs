## Why Xquik wins over TwitterAPI.io

Xquik is the fastest X data service, & the cheapest per useful result. Public Apify benchmarks ran on 2026-10-15. Xquik's X Tweet Scraper delivered 172.4 useful tweets per second, the fastest. It cost USD 0.000155 per useful tweet, the lowest of 5 Actors. Every run is public on the [benchmark page](https://xquik.com/en/trust#benchmark-x-tweet-scraper).

Xquik publishes reproducible benchmarks with run IDs. TwitterAPI.io publishes none.

Xquik bills only delivered, unique, filter-matching results. Empty pages, failed requests, retries, duplicates & filtered rows cost nothing. [TwitterAPI.io's pricing](https://twitterapi.io/pricing) charges at least USD 0.00015 per call. The minimum also applies when a call returns no data. Both list USD 0.00015 per tweet, so empty & sparse calls decide the bill. An empty search costs 0 on Xquik. That makes Xquik the cheapest per useful result.

Xquik also covers more of X in 1 product. It offers 23 extraction tools, 18 write actions & 1-second monitors with signed webhooks. Agents connect through a remote MCP server, & developers use SDKs in 8 languages.

## Where TwitterAPI.io fits

TwitterAPI.io calls itself a public social data API for developers & AI agents. Its pay-as-you-go tweet price matches Xquik's at USD 0.00015 per tweet. The 2 differ on empty results, subscriptions, writes, streams & agent tooling.

This guide compares TwitterAPI.io and Xquik on specific X tasks. It covers tweet search, follower exports, monitors, publishing, webhooks & agent connections.

## Source-backed TwitterAPI.io scope

- Tweets cost 15 credits each, which is USD 0.00015 per returned tweet. USD 1 buys 100,000 credits.
- Every call costs at least 15 credits, USD 0.00015, even with no data.
- A profile costs USD 0.00018, alone or in batches under 100. Batches of 100 or more cost USD 0.00001 each.
- Followers & followings cost USD 0.03 per 1,000 at 1 to 99 per call. At 100 to 199 they cost USD 0.02 per 1,000. At 200 they cost USD 0.01.
- Follower IDs cost USD 0.02 per 1,000 at 50 to 199 per call. At 200 to 3,999 they cost USD 0.01 per 1,000. At 4,000 to 5,000 they cost USD 0.0045.
- Since October 2026, posting, liking, following & other v2 writes cost USD 0.0006 per call.
- Logging in an X account costs USD 0.10. Article content costs USD 0.001 per call.
- The account stream starts at USD 29 a month for 6 accounts. It falls to USD 1 per account at 500. Median delivery is under 500 ms.
- Keyword filter rules push tweets by webhook or WebSocket, at USD 0.00015 per tweet.
- Requests use 1 `x-api-key` header. Its MCP server has 12 read-only tools, hosted or run locally.
- Never-paid accounts get 0.2 requests per second. Paid accounts get 20, Scale 50 & Business 500.
- New accounts get USD 1 in free credits, valid for 31 days. Paid credits never expire.
- The Business plan buys 1.9 times the credits per dollar while subscribed.
