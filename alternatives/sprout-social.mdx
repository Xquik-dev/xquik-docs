## Source-backed Sprout Social scope

Sprout Social's features page describes Smart Inbox, Contact Views, and conversation history. Brand Keywords search X by keyword, hashtag, and location. Publishing covers multiple profiles, media, ViralPost timing, approvals, and bulk scheduling. Reporting includes PDF/CSV exports, X competitor reports, and X keyword reports. The page also lists CRM integrations and chatbots.

Sprout's official publishing page describes a social media calendar, Optimal Send Times based on 16 weeks of audience data, AI caption and alt-text help, approval workflows, an asset library, URL tracking, and Campaign Planner workflows.

Sprout's analytics pages describe network, cross-network, paid, competitive, and internal reports. Named reports cover tags, post performance, competitors, case teams, and networks. The pricing pages also list Premium Analytics, Listening, Advocacy, Influencer Marketing, and Enterprise options.

Sprout's pricing page lists annual billing and a trial without a credit card. Standard costs USD 199 per seat/month with 5 profiles. Professional costs USD 299 with no listed profile cap. Advanced costs USD 399 with the Sprout API and Helpdesk integrations. Enterprise uses custom pricing with onboarding, SSO, and priority support. The page lists analytics, listening, advocacy, services, and influencer add-ons separately.

## Migration path

Keep Sprout Social for publishing, Smart Inbox, approvals, analytics, listening, care, advocacy, and influencer workflows if your team already works that way. Move one X-only task when it needs tweet results, follower files, signed webhooks, or MCP tool results.

Keep the test small: one task, one output, one cost model, and one owner for the output. Use Xquik for the X work that needs tweet search, follower export, post actions, media uploads, DMs, tweet monitoring, webhook delivery, SDK calls, or MCP.

Price the real workload. On Xquik, Starter is USD 20/month with 140,000 credits. Top-ups are USD 0.00015/credit, webhook and stored-event management are free, and active monitors bill only while enabled.
