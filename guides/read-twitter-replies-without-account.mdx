You can read replies to a public X (Twitter) post without an X account.
Paste the post URL into the
[X (Twitter) Replies Viewer](https://xquik.com/en/twitter-replies). You sign in
to Xquik, not to X.

<section>

## Read the replies to a post

<Steps>

  <Step title="Copy the post URL">

    Copy the URL of the public post whose replies you want.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into the replies viewer">

    Open the [X (Twitter) Replies Viewer](https://xquik.com/en/twitter-replies) and paste the URL.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a sort and a count">

    Pick Relevance, Latest or Likes under Sort. Then pick how many replies you want.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Extract Replies">

    Xquik lists the replies with their authors. Export CSV, Excel, JSON or Markdown.

  </Step>
</Steps>

</section>

<section>

## Sort the replies

| Sort      | What comes first            |
| --------- | --------------------------- |
| Relevance | The replies X ranks highest |
| Latest    | The newest replies          |
| Likes     | The most liked replies      |

Each row is 1 reply to the post. It shows the reply text and its author. It adds
the reply's likes, reposts and replies.

</section>

## Read replies questions

### Do I need an X account to read replies?

No. The replies viewer reads public posts without your X account. You need an
Xquik account to run it.

### How do I see the replies to a tweet?

Paste the post URL and press Extract Replies. Xquik lists the replies with their
authors.

### What does each reply include?

Each row holds the reply text, its author and engagement counts.

### Can I export tweet replies?

Yes. Download them as CSV, Excel, JSON or Markdown.

### Can I read a whole thread the same way?

Yes. Read [Twitter thread unroll](/guides/twitter-thread-unroll) to list a whole
conversation.
