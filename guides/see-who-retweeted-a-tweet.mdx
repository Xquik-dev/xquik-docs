To see who retweeted a tweet, open
[See Who Retweeted an X (Twitter) Post](https://xquik.com/en/repost-extractor).
Paste the post URL and press Extract Reposters. Xquik lists the accounts that
reposted it.

<section>

## List the accounts that retweeted a post

<Steps>

  <Step title="Copy the post URL">

    Copy the URL of the public post on X.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it and press Extract Reposters">

    Open [See Who Retweeted an X (Twitter) Post](https://xquik.com/en/repost-extractor) and paste the URL.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the list">

    Save the accounts as CSV, Excel, JSON or Markdown.

  </Step>
</Steps>

Each row is 1 account that reposted the post. It shows the username, bio and
location. It adds the follower count, following count and verified badge.

</section>

## Who retweeted questions

### What is a retweet?

X now calls a retweet a repost. It shares a post with your followers as it is.
It adds no text of its own.

### Do I need an X account to see who retweeted?

No. Xquik reads public posts without your X account. You need an Xquik account
to run it.

### Does it include quote tweets?

No. It lists plain reposts.

### Why does X hide some reposters?

X leaves out protected and suspended accounts. Their reposts don't show in public
lists.

### Can I see who liked the same tweet?

Only if you wrote it. Read
[how to see who liked my tweet](/guides/see-who-liked-my-tweet).
