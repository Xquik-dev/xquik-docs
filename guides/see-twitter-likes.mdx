To see your Twitter likes, open your profile on X and tap Likes. X shows your
likes only to you, so nobody else can open that tab.

<section>

## See your likes on X

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open your profile">

    Sign in to X and open your own profile.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Likes">

    The Likes tab lists the posts you liked.

  </Step>
</Steps>

</section>

<section>

## Search or export your likes

The [X (Twitter) Likes Viewer](https://xquik.com/en/twitter-likes-viewer) lists
your liked posts in 1 place. You can search, sort and export them there.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Connect your X account">

    The viewer reads only the X account you connect.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your username">

    The viewer lists the posts you liked.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the list">

    Save it as CSV, Excel, JSON, Markdown, PDF or plain text.

  </Step>
</Steps>

Each row is 1 post you liked. It shows the text, the author, the post date and
any media. It adds the likes, reposts, replies and views.

</section>

<section>

## Twitter likes questions

### Are Twitter likes private?

Yes. X shows your likes only to you. Other people can't open your likes tab.

### Can I see someone else's Twitter likes?

No. X keeps each account's likes private. The likes viewer reads only the
account you connect.

### Can I export my Twitter likes?

Yes. Export them to CSV, Excel, JSON, Markdown, PDF or plain text.

### Can I see who liked my own tweet?

Yes. X shows the likers of a post to its author. Read
[how to see who liked my tweet](/guides/see-who-liked-my-tweet).
