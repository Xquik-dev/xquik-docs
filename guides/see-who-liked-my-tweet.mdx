Only the author of a post can see who liked it on X. To list the likers, connect
your X account. Then paste the post URL into
[See Who Liked Your Tweet](https://xquik.com/en/see-who-liked-a-tweet).

<section>

## List the accounts that liked your tweet

<Steps>

  <Step title="Connect the account that posted it">

    X shows likers only to the author, so Xquik reads them through your account.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the post URL">

    Open [See Who Liked Your Tweet](https://xquik.com/en/see-who-liked-a-tweet) and paste the URL.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the list">

    Save the accounts as CSV, Excel, JSON or Markdown.

  </Step>
</Steps>

Each row is 1 account that liked the post. It shows the username, display name
and profile photo. It adds the follower count, following count and verified
status.

</section>

<section>

## Who liked my tweet questions

### Can you see who liked a tweet?

Only the author can. X hides the likers from everyone else.

### Why can't I see everyone who liked my tweet?

X hides some likers, even from the author. The like count can be higher than the
list. Xquik returns the likers X shows you.

### Can I see who liked someone else's tweet?

No. X shows likers only to the author. For someone else's post, read its
[replies](/guides/read-twitter-replies-without-account) or its
[reposts](/guides/see-who-retweeted-a-tweet).

### Do I need X API keys?

No. Xquik reads the likers for you. Connect your X account and paste the tweet
URL.
